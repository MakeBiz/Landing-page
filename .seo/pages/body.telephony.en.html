What to know about calls in the UAE

  • Internet telephony in the UAE is regulated by the TDRA and is legal only through licensed operators. The main telecom operators are du and e& (formerly Etisalat)
  • Voice calls over WhatsApp and other messengers are often unavailable in the UAE, so sales still happen by phone while chats run separately on WhatsApp
  • Working around restrictions with a VPN or foreign SIP numbers is not an option for business telephony: it breaks the rules, and the sales line can stop working at any moment

Three ways to connect telephony

Your PBX via the SIP connector

A cloud or office PBX with a du or e& line connects to Bitrix24 through the built-in SIP connector. You can call from the browser, the Bitrix24 app or a SIP phone, and recordings land in the CRM

PBX via a Market app

Routing and calls stay on the PBX side, and Bitrix24 receives the events, recordings and records. A fit for call centres: agents call from a softphone or SIP phone rather than the browser

A number rented in Bitrix24

For separate business lines and foreign markets: the number is rented directly in Bitrix24, sometimes with document verification. We check which countries' numbers are available before connecting
