Bitrix24 or Zoho CRM: which one for a UAE company

In the UAE the choice most often comes down to these two systems. We are a Bitrix24 integrator, so we say that upfront and look honestly at where Zoho is stronger. The decision depends less on feature lists and more on how your team, sales and accounting are set up

The main differences

Pricing model

Bitrix24 cloud is a flat price per company within the plan's user limit: add employees and the bill stays the same. Zoho CRM is priced per user per month. For a team of 10-15 people or more the difference is noticeable, for 3-5 people it is small

What is inside

Bitrix24 puts CRM, tasks and projects, a contact centre with WhatsApp and Open Channels, telephony, team chat and documents in one place. Zoho CRM is the CRM, while tasks, support and accounting come from separate apps in the Zoho ecosystem

Where the data lives

Bitrix24 has an on-premise edition for your own server with source code access. Zoho CRM runs only in the Zoho cloud

Accounting and VAT

If accounting already runs in Zoho Books, Zoho CRM and Zoho Books work together out of the box. We connect Bitrix24 to your accounting system through an API integration

Team language

For a Russian-speaking team Bitrix24 feels more familiar: interface, documentation and support in Russian. For a fully English-speaking team there is no difference

Fitting the process

Both systems customise deeply. In Bitrix24 that means Smart Processes, automation rules and business processes; in Zoho CRM, modules, Blueprint and Deluge scripts

When Zoho CRM is the better fit

  • The company already works in the Zoho ecosystem or keeps its books in Zoho Books
  • A small sales team of a few people that needs nothing beyond a CRM
  • Processes and the team are fully in English and there is no need for your own server

When Bitrix24 is the better fit

  • You want one system for sales, tasks, the contact centre and internal communication rather than a set of apps
  • Sales run through WhatsApp and phone, and everything has to meet in the client record
  • The team is growing and paying for every new user does not make sense
  • Data must stay on your own server: the on-premise edition with code access
  • Part of the team or client base speaks Russian
