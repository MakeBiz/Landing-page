Where agencies lose deals

Four gaps we find in almost every agency before the rollout

Portal leads sit in the inbox

A lead arrives by email or in the portal dashboard and the broker replies an hour later, or tomorrow. By then the buyer has messaged three other agencies

WhatsApp lives on the broker's phone

The whole client history is in the agent's personal chats. When the agent moves to another agency, the clients and the agreements leave too

Ad permits tracked in a spreadsheet

A listing stays on a portal with an expired Trakheesi permit, and the agency finds out when the ad is taken down

Commissions worked out after the deal

The agent's share, the split with a partner agency and VAT are counted by hand, so the payout dispute starts after closing

What we build in Bitrix24

Property database

A dedicated section for every listing: type, community, project and developer, ready or off-plan, price in AED, owner, photos and status (available, reserved, sold, rented). The Trakheesi permit number and expiry date sit in the property record

Portal leads in the CRM

Leads from Property Finder, Bayut and Dubizzle reach Bitrix24 through the portal integration or an app from the Bitrix24 Market. Each lead is assigned by community or property type right away, and a returning client goes to their existing record instead of a duplicate

A pipeline per deal type

Ready sales, rentals, off-plan with a developer booking and payment plan, commercial. Each has its own stages, deadlines and required fields

RERA forms by stage

Deal stages are tied to the documents: Form A with the seller to market the property, Form B with the buyer, Form F between buyer and seller, Form I with a partner agency on a shared deal. Templates fill in from the deal record

Viewings and feedback

Every viewing is a CRM activity with the date, broker and client feedback. You see which properties get viewed and not taken, and how many viewings a deal needs

Commissions in dirhams

The agent's percentage, the partner split and 5% VAT are calculated in the deal. Agent payouts are visible before closing, not after

What management gets to see

  • First response time on leads from each portal and by each broker
  • Which portal brings deals, not just leads, so the listing budget stops being spent blind
  • A reminder to the broker and the manager before a property's Trakheesi permit expires
  • The broker's BRN and RERA card expiry in the employee profile
  • All WhatsApp chats and calls in the client record, including after an agent leaves
  • A funnel for every broker: leads, viewings, forms, deals, commission

An AI agent on the first line

For agencies with a steady flow of leads we add an AI agent: it answers a portal lead within minutes, at night and at weekends too, asks about budget, community, timing, buying or renting and payment method, and hands the broker a qualified client. More on the AI agents page
