Where clinics and salons lose clients

Four places where a booking turns into an empty slot in the schedule or into a risk for the clinic

Booked but did not show up

The receptionist cannot always call everyone the day before, and a freed slot is rarely offered to anyone else in time. The doctor or stylist waits and the room stands empty

Bookings in five places

The phone, the receptionist's WhatsApp, social media messages, the website form and the paper diary at reception. Two clients end up booked for the same time, and a message that arrived at night cannot be found in the morning

Clients on a personal phone

A stylist or coordinator handles clients through their personal WhatsApp. When they move to another salon, the regular clients go with them

Health data in chats

A patient sends scans and test results over WhatsApp, and the receptionist forwards them to the doctor in a personal chat. The data ends up outside the medical system, while the clinic is responsible by law for keeping it confidential

What we build in Bitrix24

A schedule for doctors and stylists

Doctors, stylists, rooms and devices are set up in Bitrix24 Booking as resources, each with its own working hours and appointment length. A doctor and a room, or a stylist and a device, are booked together, and every receptionist sees at once which time is taken

Booking from the website and social media

There are three types of booking form: the earliest available time, all free slots on a chosen day, or a choice of specialist. The form goes on the website, into social media posts and into messenger chats, and the booking lands in the CRM straight away

Confirmations and reminders

The client gets WhatsApp messages: the booking details, a request to confirm the visit via a link (one day ahead by default), a reminder on the day and a message if they are running late. Unconfirmed bookings are highlighted, the receptionist gets an activity, and a freed slot can be offered to clients on the waitlist

Every enquiry in one record

Messages from WhatsApp (via the official API) and social media, calls and website forms arrive in Bitrix24 and are routed by branch, language or service line. Conversations run from the clinic or salon number, not from an employee's personal phone

Repeat visits

For services with a clear cycle, such as a follow-up, a touch-up, colouring or a facial, an automation rule sets the date of the next visit: the client gets a reminder if they agreed to such messages, and the receptionist gets a task. Clients who have not come in for a long time show up as a separate list

Services, payments and revenue

Services from Booking appear in the CRM product catalogue automatically, and the price and discount from the deal show up in the booking. The statistics show revenue by specialist and room, and treatment packages are tracked with the number of visits left

What lives in the CRM and what stays in the medical system

Bitrix24 does not replace the clinic's electronic medical record (EMR) system. We split the data so that the receptionist sees everything needed for booking, and medical information stays in the system built for it

In Bitrix24

Name, phone, language, branch, specialist, date and time of the visit, enquiry source, payments, consents for messages and calls, chats about the booking

admin data

In the medical system

Complaints, diagnoses, prescriptions, test results, scans, before-and-after photos, consent to treatment. In Dubai, clinics exchange health data through NABIDH, the DHA health information exchange

clinical data
  • UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the use of ICT in health fields bars storing, processing, generating or transferring outside the UAE any health information and data related to health services provided in the country, except in cases defined by a health authority decision issued in coordination with the ministry (Article 13). According to Bitrix24, bitrix24.ae cloud accounts are hosted in AWS data centres in Frankfurt
  • The law does not split data into admin and clinical, so where the CRM is hosted is decided together with the clinic's lawyer. We do not set up fields for diagnoses, test results or photos in cloud Bitrix24. Scans a patient sends over WhatsApp are kept in the chat history, so how to handle them is also agreed with the lawyer. If the lawyer treats booking data as health data too, or if the clinical side is needed in the CRM, we install on-premise Bitrix24 on your server in the UAE: the Booking module is available on-premise too
  • Health data is kept for at least 25 years from the date of the last procedure (Article 20). If it gets into the CRM, that period has to be taken into account for the CRM as well
  • Under the definition in Article 1 of the law, health information can be audible too, and on the phone patients often tell the receptionist how they feel. Where call recordings are stored (in the cloud, on the PBX or on a server in the UAE) is decided before telephony is connected. Telephony in the UAE
  • Patient information must be kept confidential and may not be used for non-health purposes without the patient's written approval, except in cases set by the law, such as an insurer audit or a request from the judicial authorities or the health authority (Article 16)
  • The personal data protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, Article 2) does not apply to health data that has its own legislation. Health data in a clinic is governed by Law No. 2 of 2019, while the client base of a beauty salon falls under the personal data law, unless the salon is in a free zone with its own data protection law
  • Clinics in Dubai Healthcare City also follow the DHCC Health Data Protection Regulation No. 7 of 2013: health data may leave DHCC only for a jurisdiction with an adequate level of protection, and only with the patient's authorisation or when it is needed for their care (Section 67)

Consents kept in the client record

Every consent is stored with its date, channel and term, and lists for broadcasts and marketing calls are built only from clients who gave consent and have not withdrawn it

WhatsApp messages

Under WhatsApp rules, a business may message people who gave it their number and agreed to receive messages, and a request to stop must be honoured whatever channel it comes through. The record shows what the consent covers: visit reminders only, or news and offers as well

WhatsApp Business Policy

Marketing calls and messages

Marketing calls are allowed from 9am to 6pm (Article 5), only from numbers registered to the company and never to numbers on the DNCR (Article 4). A client who says no on the first call is not called again; one who does not answer is called back no more than once a day and twice a week. Marketing text messages and marketing messages through social media apps count as telemarketing too (Article 1)

Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024

Photos, videos and reviews in ads

A patient's image or words may be used in an ad only with their written consent for a specific purpose and period, and for patients under 18 a parent gives separate consent. The record holds the date, purpose and term of the consent, and an automation rule reminds you when it expires and the post should come down

DHA standards 2026

Disclosure of health information

We note whom the patient has allowed in writing to receive information about them, such as a relative or an employer. Consent to treatment stays in the medical records, and we do not duplicate it in the CRM

Law No. 2 of 2019, Article 16

Ads and broadcasts without medical promises

The DHA standards for medical advertising on social media (circular CIR-2026-00000144 of 17 August 2026) cover private clinics and professionals licensed by the DHA, and WhatsApp counts as social media there too. The same rules apply to influencers who promote the clinic's services

  • Absolute and exaggerated claims are banned: "the best", "the only", "the safest", "unique", "100%", "guaranteed success", "no side effects", "immediate results", "miracle". Ads must not frighten people, play on anxiety or claim that a service is better or safer than anyone else's. The risks of a procedure have to be stated plainly
  • Before-and-after photos: the same person, the same lens, no editing, and a note that results vary from person to person
  • A post that names the clinic is approved in advance by the medical director. In Bitrix24 the approval runs as a workflow: the marketer drafts the broadcast, the director approves it, and the approved version and date stay in the history
  • Reminders that the clinic sends from its own number through the WhatsApp Business Platform go out as utility templates with no promotion. Add an offer to a reminder and Meta treats the template as marketing, so offers go out separately and only to clients who agreed to receive news and offers
  • WhatsApp rules forbid using it for telemedicine or to send or request health information if applicable regulations prohibit passing such information to systems that do not meet heightened requirements for handling it. So clinic messages carry only the date, time, branch and specialist name, with no diagnoses, test results or procedure names. The built-in Booking messages include the resource name, so rooms and devices in the schedule get neutral names
  • The WhatsApp and telemarketing rules are the same for a clinic and a beauty salon
