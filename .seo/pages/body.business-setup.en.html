Where clients and deadlines get lost

Four places where a deal goes cold, a renewal goes to a competitor and a client check turns into a paper chase

A long decision with no next step

The client compares the mainland and free zones, works out how many visas they need, talks it over with partners and disappears for weeks. Without a date for the next contact the deal goes cold, and the consultant only remembers the clients who get in touch on their own

Documents in chats and inboxes

Passports, photos, visas and statements arrive in the consultant's WhatsApp, the shared inbox and personal messages. The right file is dug out of the chat history, and a passport copy that has already expired goes to the authority

Deadlines in a spreadsheet

Licences, visas, Emirates IDs and office leases renew on different dates, and the spreadsheet falls behind. The client finds out about the expiry on their own, and the renewal goes to whoever reminded them first

Due diligence in folders

Questionnaires, document copies, beneficial owner data and records of checks sit in different places. When a bank or a supervisor asks how a client was checked, the history has to be pieced together

What we build in Bitrix24

A pipeline for a long cycle

Stages from the first enquiry to payment: consultation, choice of jurisdiction and business activity, proposal, approval, payment. Each stage has its own time limit: if a deal stalls, an automation rule gives the consultant a task to contact the client. The source and the partner who referred the client are visible in the record and in reports

Proposals and contracts from templates

The document generator builds the proposal, contract and invoice from your own DOCX templates with CRM data: the package, government fees and your services on separate lines. The client gets the document as a link by SMS, as a PDF by email or in a chat if they have written to you before

Client documents in the record

The client uploads their passport, photo and other documents through a CRM form with file fields, and the files land in the record straight away. Passport, visa and Emirates ID expiry dates are kept in separate fields, and the key fields are required by stage: without a passport copy the deal cannot be handed over for formation

Formation as a separate process

Once the client has paid, they move into a Formation smart process with its own stages, for example: trade name reservation, initial approval, licence, establishment card, visas, bank account. Tasks for each step are created automatically, and the client gets a message about what is ready and what is needed from them

Renewals by date

The licence, establishment card, visas, Emirates IDs and office lease are recorded with their expiry dates in a Renewals smart process linked to the client company and its people. Ahead of time, for example 60 and 30 days before, an automation rule gives the consultant a renewal task and sends the client a WhatsApp reminder if they have agreed to such messages

Recurring services

Bookkeeping, VAT and PRO services under contract run as recurring deals: the CRM creates a deal for each period on its own, and the invoice is prepared from a template. The client record shows all of their deals, not just the first registration

Due diligence and beneficial owners

Under UAE anti-money laundering rules, corporate service providers that set up companies for clients, give them a registered address or take roles in their companies must check their clients and the clients' beneficial owners. Bitrix24 does not screen clients against sanctions lists, but we set it up so that the full history of the check is in the client record and the deal does not go to formation until the check is passed

  • Firms that, for their clients, act as an agent in setting up legal persons, act or arrange for someone else to act as a director, secretary or partner, provide a registered office or correspondence address, or act as a trustee or nominee shareholder count as DNFBPs (designated non-financial businesses and professions). This is set out in Article 3 of Cabinet Resolution No. 134 of 2025, the executive regulations of Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025
  • The identity of the client and the beneficial owner is verified before or while the business relationship is established (Article 6), and if due diligence cannot be done, the relationship must not start or continue (Article 14). That is why we set up the deal in Bitrix24 so that it cannot be handed over for formation until the record shows that the check has been passed
  • A beneficial owner is a person who, directly or through other companies, holds 25% or more of the capital or voting rights, or controls the company in another way, for example through the right to appoint or remove most of the board. If no such person can be identified, a senior manager is treated as the beneficial owner (Article 5 of Cabinet Resolution No. 109 of 2023)
  • The company keeps a register of beneficial owners and a register of partners or shareholders (Articles 8 and 10 of the same resolution). The beneficial owner register includes the passport or ID number and its expiry date, and a valid passport or ID copy is attached to the register of partners or shareholders (Article 10). So the passports of beneficial owners and shareholders go into Renewals too, and ahead of time, for example a month before expiry, an automation rule sets a task to request a new copy and update the data
  • The law requires ongoing monitoring, not just a check at onboarding (Article 19 of Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025). The record holds the risk level set by the compliance officer, while the date of the next review goes into Renewals, where an automation rule sends a reminder ahead of it
  • Due diligence documents and data are kept for at least 5 years from the end of the business relationship or the completion of the transaction (Article 25 of Resolution No. 134 of 2025). A former client's record is not deleted: their deals stay in the CRM at closed stages, and only the people responsible can open the record
  • Suspicious transactions are reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit immediately and without delay through its electronic system (Article 18 of Resolution No. 134 of 2025); in the UAE that system is goAML. We do not keep these reports in the CRM: the compliance officer prepares and stores them outside the shared system
  • Fields with passport data and check results are visible only to selected employees and departments, and sales managers work only with their own deals. Account administrators see every field, so we keep the circle of administrators small
  • According to Bitrix24, bitrix24.ae cloud accounts are hosted in AWS data centres in Frankfurt. If your retention policy or your partners require document copies to stay in the UAE, we install on-premise Bitrix24 on your server in the UAE
  • For breaches of anti-money laundering rules, the supervisor can impose a fine of AED 10,000 to AED 5,000,000 for each violation (Article 17 of Federal Decree-Law No. 10 of 2025)

Client deadlines the CRM keeps track of

Dates from licences and client documents turn into tasks and reminders. Some of these deadlines are set by law, and missing them can cost the client a penalty

Corporate tax registration

A company incorporated on or after 1 March 2024 applies for registration within 3 months of incorporation. Late registration carries an AED 10,000 penalty, but the FTA waives it, or refunds it to the tax account, if the first tax return is filed within 7 months of the end of the first tax period. The deadline is counted from the incorporation date in the record

FTA Decision No. 3 and Cabinet Decision No. 10 of 2024

Beneficial owner registers

The registers go to the registrar within 60 days of licensing and registration, and changes to the data are reported within 15 days. An automation rule sets tasks with these deadlines: from the licence date and from the date of the change, which the consultant marks in the record

Cabinet Resolution No. 109 of 2023, Articles 11 and 15

Licence and establishment card
