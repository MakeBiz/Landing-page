UAE corporate tax: the CRM data your return relies on

A CRM is not your accounting system, but it holds the raw material behind the revenue in a UAE corporate tax return: deals, invoices, contracts and client details. The Federal Tax Authority requires records to be kept for at least 7 years after the end of the tax period, and the return and payment are due within 9 months: a financial year that closed on 31 December 2025 had to be filed by 30 September 2026. For calendar-year companies the 2026 return is due by 30 September 2027, and the data behind it is already building up in the CRM.

What the rules say

Under the FTA Corporate Tax guide, the first AED 375,000 of taxable income is taxed at 0% and anything above it at 9%. The FTA notice of 27 August 2025 lists the records you must keep: transactions for the period, assets, liabilities and shareholdings. Without them the FTA cannot verify taxable income, and missing records carry administrative penalties.

Small businesses have Small Business Relief: with revenue of AED 3 million or less, a company can elect the simplified treatment. On 7 August 2026 the Ministry of Finance announced Ministerial Decision No. 131, which extends the relief to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029. The threshold is based on revenue, the relief must be elected in the return, and record keeping still applies.

The CRM data worth getting right

  • The legal entity on every deal. If one portal serves a free zone company and a mainland company, revenue has to split by entity, as covered in our piece on free zone and mainland in one Bitrix24.
  • Invoice and payment next to the deal. Number, date, amount, VAT and payment status, not just a deal value at the "Won" stage.
  • Client details. Legal name, TRN and country, one record per counterparty with no duplicates.
  • Contracts and supporting documents. Stored in the record, not in a rep's inbox: you may be asked for them years later.
  • Currency. USD or EUR deals with the rate used to convert to AED on the invoice date.

Where the gaps usually are

A deal is closed in the CRM while the invoice lives in the accounting system with no link between them. Annual revenue in the CRM and in the books does not match, and the only way to find out why is by hand. A rep leaves and the contracts leave with their email. A new report will not fix any of this; connecting the CRM to accounting will. The exchange is described on the Bitrix24 integrations page, and VAT invoicing on the VAT and e-invoicing page. Your accountant or tax agent decides the tax position; our job is to make sure the data they work from is complete.

What it means for your budget

At 9%, a revenue error turns straight into money: an extra AED 100,000 reported above the AED 375,000 band means AED 9,000 more tax. For a company with revenue close to AED 3 million, clean records show whether it actually meets the relief conditions. Quick answers

How long must UAE corporate tax records be kept? At least 7 years after the end of the tax period they relate to.

When is the corporate tax return due? Within 9 months after the end of the tax period, and the tax is paid by the same date.

Does Small Business Relief continue after 2026? Yes. Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 extends it to tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029.
