AI agent in Arabic and English: setup and cost

A second language does not turn an AI agent into a separate purchase: one agent on the Start package costs AED 5,000 once plus maintenance at 10% of the implementation cost a month, and there is no "Arabic" line on the price list. What grows is the work: the knowledge base, message templates and tests all have to exist in two languages. In Dubai, bilingual is now the baseline: every one of the 22 agents on the new Dubai Chambers marketplace works in Arabic and English.

What Dubai Chambers launched

On 26 October Dubai Chambers opened Chamber AI Hub, a marketplace of ready-made AI agents for companies operating in Dubai (launch announcement on TradeArabia). It starts with 22 agents across seven functions: compliance, procurement, operations, finance, sales, HR and logistics, with a target of 100 within two years. Every agent works in Arabic and English, data is hosted in the UAE, and any agent can be tried in a sandbox before it is switched on. New users get complimentary credits, then move to paid usage-based packages. The announcement does not give package prices.

On 16 November Saeed Al Gergawi, Vice President of the Dubai Chamber of Digital Economy, restated the goal of the two-year programme in a column for Gulf News: more than 295,000 private sector companies. For businesses in the UAE the signal is plain: customers and partners will soon expect any agent to answer in their language, not only in English.

What a second language changes in the setup

The knowledge base. The answers, terms and prices the agent relies on have to live in two versions and be updated together. Otherwise the English side already knows the new prices while the Arabic side quotes the old ones.

WhatsApp templates. Outside the 24-hour window you can only message a customer with a template, and Meta approves each template separately for every language. Connecting WhatsApp to the portal is covered on the Bitrix24 messengers page.

Testing. Chats in the UAE often mix Arabic and English in a single message, so the agent should be tested on real conversations like these, not on clean Modern Standard Arabic.

Handover to a person. You need a rule for who takes an Arabic conversation when only one manager on the team speaks Arabic, and a language field on the customer record so the next reply goes out in the same language.

A catalogue agent or your own inside the CRM

A catalogue agent handles a standard task and is paid for by usage. If the task does not need your deals or customer history, it is a sensible first step: the sandbox and starter credits let you test the idea without spending. If the agent has to quote your prices, see the deal and log the outcome on the record, it has to live inside the CRM. The roles such agents cover are listed on the AI agents page.

What it means for your budget

Your own agent on the Start package is AED 5,000 once and AED 500 a month for maintenance, AED 11,000 in the first year. Connecting WhatsApp is AED 500, with the provider subscription billed separately. Work beyond the package is charged at AED 200 an hour, and every figure is on the pricing page. How to check whether an agent pays for itself is covered in our piece on AI agent payback.

The real saving on a second language comes from the order of work. Build the answer base in one language first, test it on real conversations, and only then translate: fixing one version is cheaper than fixing two. Submit your Arabic WhatsApp templates for approval early, so the launch does not end up waiting on Meta.
