UAE business telephony for your CRM: check this first

Before you hook a phone line up to your CRM in the UAE, get five answers straight: who your carrier is, where your PBX runs, how calls will reach Bitrix24, how the system handles the UAE working week, and who will actually review the recordings. The regulator sets the hard limit. Under TDRA's VoIP policy, only two licensed operators, du and e&, may provide voice over IP on public networks, so your numbers and lines come from them and the CRM connects on top.

The TDRA rule in plain terms

TDRA's VoIP Regulatory Policy (version 2.0, issued 30 December 2009, available on the TDRA website) says you need a licence to provide VoIP services in the country, and there are currently two licensees. A licensee may block any VoIP service running over its network without a licence (clause 3.2). The only carve-out is calling within a closed user group, such as staff of a single company talking to each other (clauses 3.3 and 3.4).

What this means for a sales team: a foreign SIP number or calls routed through a VPN might work for a month and then stop without warning. For the number your customers dial, that risk is not worth taking.

A pre-launch checklist

  • Contract and line. Whose name is on the du or e& contract, and do you have a SIP trunk or just desk phones? That decides whether the number can be connected directly.
  • PBX. A carrier-hosted cloud PBX, an on-site system, or nothing yet. Bitrix24 connects to any of them through its built-in SIP connector or a Marketplace app.
  • Devices. Office staff are fine calling from the browser; a call centre and field reps are better served by a SIP handset or softphone.
  • Hours. IVR, forwarding and after-hours handling should follow the Saturday and Sunday weekend and UAE public holidays.
  • Recordings. Calls land in the customer record, so access to them deserves the same care as access to deals. Our piece on CRM access rights covers the roles.

Selling into another country? You can rent a local number for that market inside Bitrix24, but number availability and paperwork vary by country, so check them before go-live. All three connection setups are laid out on our Bitrix24 telephony page.

The real savings aren't in the call rates. Once every call is tied to a deal, a missed inbound call turns straight into a task, and the manager can see who called back and when. Without that, the ad spend that generated the call simply leaks away. And rebuilding your setup after a number gets blocked costs more than choosing the right route on day one.

Quick answers

Can I connect a foreign SIP number to my CRM in the UAE? Technically yes, but under TDRA policy the carrier may block unlicensed VoIP. A du or e& line is the safer choice for a sales number.
