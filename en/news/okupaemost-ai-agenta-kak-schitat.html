AI agent payback: run the numbers yourself

One AI agent costs AED 5,000 once plus maintenance of 10% of the implementation cost per month, which comes to AED 11,000 in the first year, or about AED 917 a month. That is the payback threshold: below it the agent loses money. Working it out takes three numbers from your own portal, and it belongs before the contract, not after.

What the 4 November survey showed

On 4 November the Oliver Wyman Forum published the UAE part of its 300,000 Voices study (the Oliver Wyman report). The numbers: 85% of employees in the UAE use AI at work, 48% of them daily, and only 3% never use it. Yet just 6% of frontline staff say their company has a clear AI vision, against 26% of executives, and 67% worry the technology will take their job.

For a payback calculation the gap between 85% and 6% is what matters. Staff already work with AI, but in private chat windows. None of that lands in the CRM, so none of the saving shows up in a company report. An agent differs here: it lives inside the portal, and every action leaves a record you can price.

The three numbers payback rests on

First, how many enquiries a month reach the task you are handing to the agent. Pull it from the CRM by lead source, not from memory.

Second, how many of them you lose today: enquiries with no reply, replies later than an hour, deals with no next step. That is the part an agent moves; it will not improve the rest.

Third, the average margin on one deal, margin and not invoice value: with invoice value the sum comes out beautiful and wrong.

Then it is arithmetic. The threshold is AED 917 a month. At an average margin of AED 2,000 per deal the agent needs to recover one deal every two months. At AED 300 it needs three deals a month, and the question stops being about the agent: it is whether the task loses enough enquiries to supply them.

What it means for your budget

The Start package is one agent at AED 5,000 once plus AED 500 a month. The first year is AED 11,000, the second only AED 6,000, which is why the one year view is the strict one. The Business package, a team of agents, is AED 10,000 once and AED 1,000 a month, lifting the threshold to AED 1,833. For scale, AED 5,000 buys 25 hours of work at our rate of AED 200 an hour, and support starts at AED 1,250 a month for five hours. Figures are on the pricing page.

The decision rule is short. If lost enquiries multiplied by your margin do not clear AED 917 a month, an agent is not the first thing to buy: fix the process by hand and come back when the volume grows. If they clear it several times over, there is no reason to wait. We run a demo on your process free of charge, 30 to 40 minutes, and the calculation fits inside that call.

If the portal does not hold those numbers

Then the first purchase is measurement, not an agent. Make lead source, first response time and loss reason required fields, then add a manager dashboard: BI analytics from AED 300 a month. A month later you have real figures, and the choice stops being an argument about faith in AI. The roles are listed on the AI agents page; sales teams most often start with the AI sales head.
