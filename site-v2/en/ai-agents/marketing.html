The agent plans, writes and publishes expert content for your company every week, then tracks how it performs in search and in AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity

The MakeBiz marketing agent finds the questions your customers ask in search and in AI chats, drafts articles and news in English and Russian, publishes them on your site with structured data and checks whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity name your brand for your key queries.

What the agent does

Topic research

Finds the questions your customers ask in search and in AI chats, groups them into a content plan and shows the demand behind each topic

Articles and news

Drafts long-form articles, news and FAQ answers in your tone of voice, in English and Russian, with sources and internal links

Publishing

Puts approved texts on your site on a schedule, with titles, meta tags, structured data and images, and pings search engines so pages are indexed fast

GEO, visibility in AI answers

Writes in the answer-first format AI assistants quote, keeps llms.txt and schema up to date and checks whether your brand appears in AI answers

Dashboards

One screen with traffic, positions, indexed pages, leads from content and AI mentions, so you see which topics bring customers

Control stays with you

Nothing goes live without approval if you want it that way; every text can be edited before publishing

Cabinet

What management sees

Screens from our own cabinet on demo data, Russian interface. You get your own numbers

  • Indexed pages and new pages
  • Mentions in AI assistants by month
  • Leads traced back to the topic that brought them

Questions and answers

Do we have to rebuild the site?

No. New content is added to what you have, the main site stays untouched.

What is GEO?

Visibility in AI assistant answers: so that ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity name your company when a client asks about your niche.

Will the texts be in English?

Yes, in English and Russian, in your tone of voice. Every text can be edited before publishing.

How do we know it works?

Every number in the dashboard has a source: search, site analytics, CRM. You see how many leads and which topic brought them.
