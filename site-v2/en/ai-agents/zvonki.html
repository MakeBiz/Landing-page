Questions and answers

How much does speech analytics cost?

Monthly by call volume: AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes, AED 1,700 for 10,000 minutes, from AED 4,000 for 30,000 minutes and more. Connection is free.

What languages are calls analyzed in?

English and Russian, Arabic on request. Reports and recommendations in the language of management.

Which telephony does it work with?

With telephony connected to Bitrix24 and most cloud PBXs that provide call recordings.

What criteria are calls scored by?

By your script: stages, required phrases, objection handling. Criteria are set at the start and adjusted over time.

Is there an implementation example?

Yes, the HAVAL dealer network: over 300,000 call minutes a month against 40+ criteria. The case is in the cases section.
