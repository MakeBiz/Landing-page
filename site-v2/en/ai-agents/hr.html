Questions and answers

Do candidates drop off because of the bot?

The bot takes 20-30 minutes at a convenient time, without waiting for a call. You see how many started the test and how many finished.

Can questions be set for our vacancy?

Yes, questions, tests and scoring criteria are set for each vacancy, in English or Russian.

Which job boards does it work with?

Responses from any board and your site go through the bot by link. A direct integration with a specific board is connected on request.
