Questions and answers

Does Bitrix24 have built-in AI?

Yes, CoPilot: in the CRM it transcribes and summarises calls, fills in fields and scores calls. Usage depends on the plan.

How much does an AI-powered CRM cost in the UAE?

CRM setup from AED 10,000, an AI agent from AED 5,000 plus 10% a month, speech analytics from AED 1,000 a month, licence separate.

How is an AI agent different from CoPilot?

CoPilot helps an employee in the record. An agent works on its own: replies, leads the dialogue to a booking and sets a task for the rep.

Which languages does the AI work in?

CoPilot uses the language of the call, our agents reply in English and Russian.
