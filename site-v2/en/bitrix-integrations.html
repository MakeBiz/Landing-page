Questions and answers

Which accounting systems do you integrate with Bitrix24?

In the UAE most often Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Odoo, some companies use 1C. We also connect bank statements, warehouse and internal services via API.

What data is exchanged?

Clients and companies, products and prices, VAT invoices, payments and stock. The scope and direction of the exchange are defined in the survey.

How much does integration cost?

Integrating Bitrix24 with an accounting system starts from AED 10,000. The exact price depends on the system and exchange scope, the estimate is fixed after the survey.

Is 5% VAT handled?

Yes. Tax rates, company TRNs and VAT invoices pass between systems consistently, so accounting does not fix documents by hand.

What if the exchange breaks after an update?

We take the integration on support: we monitor the exchange and fix it within the hour package.
