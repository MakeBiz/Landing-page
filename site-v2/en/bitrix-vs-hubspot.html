Both are good systems. The difference is the pricing model, what is included and how many extra tools you end up buying around the CRM

Questions and answers

Which is cheaper for a UAE company, Bitrix24 or HubSpot?

For a team of 10 or more it is usually Bitrix24: it is priced per company, while HubSpot charges per seat plus a required onboarding on Professional and Enterprise. For a small marketing team the gap is smaller.

Can you move data from HubSpot to Bitrix24?

Yes: companies, contacts, deals and history. We estimate scope after the export, the work is AED 200 per hour.

Does Bitrix24 support 5% VAT invoices?

Yes, products with tax and the invoice template are part of the standard setup. For accounting we connect your accounting system.
