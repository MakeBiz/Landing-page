When you need on-premise

If two or more of these apply, the cloud will feel tight

Data only in-house

Security policy requires keeping clients and documents in your perimeter or in the UAE

Code customization

Custom modules, non-standard entities, logic that an app cannot do

Many internal systems

Your own ERP, accounting, telephony and services that need deep exchange

Hundreds of employees

With many users an on-premise license costs less than a subscription

Branches and holdings

Free zone and mainland companies, different entities and permissions in one system

Control over updates

You decide when to update the system

Cloud or on-premise

Cloud

  • Launch in a day, no server needed
  • Updates and backups by Bitrix24
  • Customization via apps and webhooks
  • Monthly or annual subscription

On-premise

  • Your server or data center, data in your perimeter
  • Code access and customization of any depth
  • Direct integrations with internal systems
  • Annual license, cheaper with a large team

Questions and answers

How is on-premise Bitrix24 different from cloud?

On-premise runs on your server or in your data center: data stays with you, you get code access, can customize deeply and connect internal services. Cloud runs on Bitrix24 servers, it is faster to launch, but customization is limited to apps.

How much does on-premise Bitrix24 cost?

License at bitrix24.ae prices as of October 23, 2026: Business for 50 users AED 13,184 a year ($3,590), with options for 100, 250 and 500 users. Enterprise from 1,000 users AED 91,776 ($24,990). Implementation is charged at AED 200 per hour, the estimate is fixed after a survey.

What server is needed?

Your own physical server, a cloud virtual machine or a data center in the UAE. We size the configuration for your users, deploy the environment and set up backups.

Can a cloud portal be moved to on-premise?

Yes. We migrate CRM, tasks, users and files, rebuild what does not move automatically and check the data before switching the team.

Who will update and maintain it?

We can take it on support: updates, backups, monitoring, security and improvements. Packages from AED 1,250 a month.
