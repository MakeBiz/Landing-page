How is my client registered?

By name and company at the moment of introduction. The client is assigned to you for 6 months and all their payments in that period count as yours.

Then we say so honestly before work starts. We do not drag out disputes.

Do I need to know CRM and AI?

No. You introduce, we do all the work from the meeting to implementation.

When do I get paid?

After the client pays, in dirhams to your account.

Yes, a simple partner agreement with MakeBiz Technologies FZE LLC, for an individual or a company. We send the template before the start.

Are there targets or obligations?

No. One client a year is fine too.
