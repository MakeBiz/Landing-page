CRM for a car service centre in the UAE: bookings, servicing and WhatsApp

A garage rarely loses money in the bay. It loses it before: a missed call, a booking in a notebook, a customer nobody reminded about their next service. That is exactly where a CRM helps.

A car service centre in the UAE needs a CRM for four things: bookings, a vehicle record with service history, WhatsApp service reminders and call control. In Bitrix24 this is covered by standard setup from MakeBiz Technologies starting at AED 10,000 and going live from 14 days. Connecting WhatsApp is AED 500, and call speech analytics starts at AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes.

A garage loses customers before the car reaches the bay

A service centre makes its money in the bay and loses it earlier. A call at rush hour goes unanswered. A WhatsApp message gets read and forgotten. A booking is scribbled in a notebook, and the technician finds out about the car when it is already at the gate. And the customer who came in six months ago goes elsewhere for the next service because nobody reminded them.

A CRM for a car service centre closes exactly these gaps: every enquiry lands in the system, every booking has a time slot and a bay, and every car has a service history and a next visit date. Here is the customer journey and what the CRM does at each step.

The vehicle record matters more than the customer record

A garage works with cars, not just people. One customer may own two cars, and a corporate fleet can have dozens of vehicles and several drivers. That is why in Bitrix24 the vehicle gets its own smart process: make, model, year, VIN, plate number, mileage, last service date and the full history of job cards.

The car is linked to a person or a company, so the service advisor types in a phone number and instantly sees which car it is, what was replaced last time and which work the customer postponed. How smart processes work and when you need them is covered in our guide to Bitrix24 smart processes, and custom work is on the Bitrix24 development page.

Bookings and bay capacity live in one calendar

While bookings sit in a notebook or a group chat, double-booking a bay is only a matter of time. In the CRM each bay and technician has a calendar, and every visit is tied to a car and a job. The owner sees the week ahead and knows where the free slots are.

An online form on your website creates a booking request, the service advisor confirms the slot, and the customer gets a WhatsApp message. The day before, the CRM sends a reminder, which cuts down on no-shows. Walk-ins go into the CRM too: the advisor creates the visit at check-in, so the car keeps its history and your capacity reports reflect reality.

Service reminders bring customers back

A scheduled service is a visit you know about in advance. The CRM works out the next service date from the last visit or the mileage, and a couple of weeks ahead it creates a task for the advisor and sends the customer a reminder.

Split reminders by type: routine service, an AC check before summer, a tyre change. Each type gets its own template, timing and owner.

These messages need the official WhatsApp channel. According to Meta's pricing documentation, outside the 24-hour window after a customer's message you can only send a template approved by Meta in advance, so service reminders, booking confirmations and invoices go out as templates. We connect WhatsApp to Bitrix24 for AED 500, with the provider subscription and Meta message fees on top. The scope is on the WhatsApp and messengers page.

Calls and WhatsApp end up in one record

Telephony connects to the CRM: an incoming call opens the customer and vehicle record, and a missed call turns straight into a call-back task. What to check with your carrier first is covered in our news piece on telephony for CRM in the UAE.

Answering is only half of it. What matters is how the advisor handles the call: did they quote a price, offer a slot, book the car? The call agent scores every conversation, and call speech analytics costs AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes or AED 1,700 for 10,000. The AI sales manager agent replies on WhatsApp when the advisor is busy, see our news piece on the AI agent in WhatsApp.

Questions and answers

Can Bitrix24 keep a car's service history?

Yes. The vehicle gets a smart process with VIN, plate number, mileage and job card history. It is linked to a person or company, so the history comes up from a phone number.

How do service reminders work?

The CRM calculates the next service from the last visit or mileage and creates a task for the advisor ahead of time. The customer gets a Meta-approved WhatsApp template, because free text cannot be sent outside the 24-hour window.

How much does a CRM for a garage cost?

Standard Bitrix24 setup with MakeBiz Technologies starts at AED 10,000, and WhatsApp is AED 500. The Standard license is AED 364 a month billed annually, bitrix24.ae prices as of 23 October 2026.

Can we monitor service advisors' calls?

Yes. Telephony connects to the CRM and missed calls become tasks. Call speech analytics scores every conversation, from AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes.

Does it work for fleet customers?

Yes. All vehicles and drivers are linked to the company, so job cards are visible across the fleet. Invoices can flow into Zoho Books, QuickBooks or Odoo, with integration from AED 10,000.
