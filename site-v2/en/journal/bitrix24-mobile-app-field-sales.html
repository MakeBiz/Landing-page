Bitrix24 mobile app for field sales in the UAE

Sales reps, brokers and key account managers in the UAE spend the day in cars and client offices. If the CRM only lives on a laptop, meeting outcomes arrive late or never.

The Bitrix24 mobile app is free on iOS and Android, and what it can do depends on your portal plan: cloud Bitrix24 in the UAE starts at AED 180 a month on annual billing. From the phone, a rep can see the client history, call from the record, move the deal stage, plan the next activity and send an invoice. The record and stages are set up for the phone as part of a setup from AED 10,000, or in a few hours at AED 200 an hour in a live portal.

MakeBiz Technologies · 24 November 2026 · 7 min read

The Bitrix24 app is free, your plan sets what it can do

The Bitrix24 mobile app runs on iOS and Android and installs from the App Store, Google Play and Huawei AppGallery. Bitrix24 says it is free for unlimited users, but the features inside depend on your portal plan: if a feature is not in your plan, it is not in the app either.

For a field team, that means the decision starts with the licence. Cloud Basic at AED 180 a month on annual billing covers 5 users, Standard at AED 364 covers 50, and Professional at AED 731 covers 100 and adds sales automation. Sizing is covered in the guide to plans for 10, 25 and 50 users, vendor prices in dirhams on the Bitrix24 licences page.

The deal record is trimmed down for the phone

Field reps skip the CRM because of the record, not the app. If a deal has 30 fields and half are required, nobody fills them in from a car park in Al Quoz. For field work the first screen holds only what matters before and after a visit; billing details and documents are handled by the office or the accounting integration.

Every stage should read without a manual: "Visit done" and "Waiting for order" are clear between meetings, "Qualification 2" is not. And after a visit, the deal always gets a next activity with a date, which is how a manager sees that no client is forgotten.

Calls and WhatsApp from the phone have to land in the CRM

In the UAE, clients message more than they call. If a rep chats from a personal WhatsApp, the company has no idea what was promised, so WhatsApp is connected to Bitrix24 through an open channel and the conversation shows up in the client record. Connection is AED 500 plus the provider subscription, see the Bitrix24 messengers page.

A call placed from the record is saved in the history, and the recording lands in the deal when the call goes through company telephony connected to the portal. Calls from a personal SIM are best ruled out by policy. How to notify clients about recording under UAE law is explained in the guide to call recording and consent, and line setup options are on the Bitrix24 telephony page.

Managers see field work through activities and stages

The "where I went today" message in a group chat is replaced by CRM data: the day's visits, which are closed with an outcome, which deals have no next step. Reps see their own clients and team leads their whole team, as explained in our news on CRM access rights.

In our Unilin case, travelling sales managers run regions and retail points from the phone on on-premise Bitrix24, and leadership follows the outlets in dashboards. The project is in Russia, but the field setup carries over to the UAE.

What the app does not replace

We found no offline mode and no route tracking through the day in the app description on bitrix24.com. The start-of-day check-in is not tracking of every visit. Where signal drops, the outcome goes in right after leaving the site, under a "same-day outcome" rule. Complex quotes and documents are better built in the office; the app is fine for opening and approving them.

What it costs to get the field team running

The app is free; you pay for the licence and setup. The short record, stages, the "Visit" activity, the invoice template and access rights are part of a standard Bitrix24 setup from AED 10,000. In a portal that is already live, it is usually a few hours at AED 200 an hour. WhatsApp connection is AED 500, and training the field team runs at AED 200 an hour, see the Bitrix24 training page. All prices are on the pricing page.

Illustrative example: a distributor with six sales reps

Picture a Dubai company supplying shops across the emirates. Six reps log visits in a notebook and send orders to the office on WhatsApp. After the setup, each rep has the day's visits, a short outlet record and a call button on the phone. An outcome is a stage plus a next activity, and the order goes out as an invoice from the deal with 5% VAT. Eight users including the office fit into the Standard plan at AED 364 a month. In the morning, the manager checks the list of deals with no next activity instead of scrolling the chat.

Questions and answers

How much does the Bitrix24 mobile app cost?

The app is free on iOS and Android. You pay for the portal plan, with cloud Bitrix24 from AED 180 a month on annual billing, and the features available in the app depend on that plan.

Can reps work with deals from the phone?

Yes. Reps create and edit deals, change stages, plan activities, view client history and issue invoices. The record is trimmed to the essentials so this stays quick.

Are calls from the mobile app recorded?

Call history from the record is saved automatically. The recording is attached to the deal when the call goes through company telephony connected to Bitrix24.

Does the app work without internet?

The app description on bitrix24.com does not mention an offline mode. If signal drops on site, the visit outcome goes in right after leaving, and that is written into the sales policy.

Can a manager see where a rep is?

The app has a start-of-day check-in with time, location and a photo. Route tracking is not in the description, so field work is managed through activities and stages.
