Which Bitrix24 plan for 10, 25 or 50 users

Cloud Bitrix24 is not priced per user but per portal, with a user cap. So for 10, 25 and 50 people the answer is often the same plan, and the real question is which features you need.

For a team of 10, 25 or 50 people in the UAE, Standard usually fits: AED 364 a month billed annually for the whole portal of up to 50 users, or AED 4,368 a year. Basic at AED 180 covers only 5 people, and Professional at AED 731 a month (up to 100 users) is worth it when you need sales automation, call tracking and analytics tools. Prices from bitrix24.ae in US dollars, converted at AED 3.6725, checked on 10 November 2026.

You pay for the portal, not for each person

A cloud Bitrix24 plan has a fixed price and a user cap: Basic 5, Standard 50, Professional 100, Enterprise 250 and up. A team of 10 pays the same for Standard as a team of 50, so the cost per person drops as you grow. The Free plan covers 1 to 2 users and is only good for a first look at the system.

Basic only fits a very small team

Basic is AED 180 a month billed annually and includes sales pipelines, recurring deals, online payments, and email, telephony and WhatsApp integration. But the cap is 5 users, so a sixth person will not fit. For a team of 10, Basic is off the table even if the features are enough.

It makes sense when the CRM is for the owner and two or three reps and the team will not grow within a year. If growth is planned, price Standard from the start: moving up a plan keeps your data, but permissions and automation for the bigger team still need a review.

Standard covers most teams up to 50 people

On bitrix24.ae, Standard adds to Basic unlimited leads, invoices and estimates, a CRM form designer, e-signature, WhatsApp marketing, task KPIs, unlimited projects and 100 GB of storage. For a sales team that keeps clients, deals, chats and invoices in one place, that is usually enough.

The threshold is simple: Standard tops out at 50 users. If headcount reaches 55 in six months you will have to move to Professional, so plan the licence a year ahead, not around today's team.

Count everyone who will log in to the portal: not just sales reps but managers, accounts, the warehouse and assistants. A common mistake is to size the plan by the sales team and then find that the whole company wants tasks and chats.

Professional is about automation, not headcount

Professional is AED 731 a month billed annually and holds up to 100 users. This is where the plan list first shows sales automation, call tracking, email marketing, ad automation, booking, analytics and reporting tools, task time tracking, task automation, RPA, HR tools and 1 TB of storage.

A team of 25 needs Professional when the process runs on automated scenarios: deals move through stages on their own, calls are tied to ad sources, the manager reads funnel reports. Which of your scenarios work on Standard is something we test on a demo portal before you buy, not after.

Enterprise starts at 250 users and AED 1,465 a month billed annually. Its list adds unlimited branch accounts, central administration, multi-level permissions, Active Directory integration and login history. For a team of up to 100 it is usually more than you need.

Annual billing saves about a third

Billed monthly, Standard is AED 529 ($144) and Professional AED 1,061 ($289). Billed annually, Standard comes to AED 4,368 a year against AED 6,348 monthly, a difference of AED 1,980 or about 31%. Once a team has decided on Bitrix24, paying for the year is almost always the better deal.

The vendor price page also lists Vibe+ versions of the plans with extended AI features and REST API access, priced higher than the regular ones. Whether you need them depends on your tasks: a standard sales CRM usually does not.

A worked example: a company grows from 12 to 60 people

A hypothetical distributor in Sharjah starts with 12 users on Standard: AED 4,368 a year for the licence. A year later it has 60 people, the Standard cap is exceeded, and it moves to Professional at AED 8,772 a year. Data and settings carry over when you move up a plan, and with the higher cap come sales automation and reports the funnel can be rebuilt around.

Questions and answers

Which Bitrix24 plan should I choose for 10 users?

Standard, because Basic holds only 5 people. Standard costs AED 364 a month billed annually for the whole portal of up to 50 users.

How much is Bitrix24 for 50 users in the UAE?

Standard for 50 users is AED 364 a month billed annually, about AED 4,368 a year. If you need sales automation, Professional is AED 731 a month.

Is Bitrix24 priced per user?

No. A cloud plan is priced for the whole portal and includes a user cap: Basic 5, Standard 50, Professional 100, Enterprise 250 and up.

What happens if we have more than 50 people?

Standard tops out at 50 users, after that you need Professional for up to 100 or Enterprise from 250. Your data carries over when you move up a plan.
