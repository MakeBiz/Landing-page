AI automation beyond sales: finance, procurement and hiring

Most talk about AI in business is about sales: reply to the lead, score the call. Yet a lot of repetitive work lives in the back office, and agents take the grind out of it there too. Here is what each department can hand to an agent and what stays with people.

Beyond sales, AI agents take over repetitive work in finance, procurement and hiring: a view of cash and risk by business line, supplier requests and quote comparison, and the first interview with a candidate. At MakeBiz Technologies one such agent costs AED 5,000 one-off, a team of agents for several departments AED 10,000, and support is 10% of the implementation fee per month.

The back office runs on repeatable work

Every week your finance person pulls numbers from the accounting system and the CRM into one spreadsheet. Your buyer sends the same request to five suppliers and then copies their answers by hand. Your recruiter asks the first candidates the same ten questions. That is template work, and an agent can take the longest part of it while leaving the decision with a person.

The finance agent builds the cash picture

A managing director wants revenue, profit, costs and cash runway for every business line on one screen, not a report at month end. The finance agent builds that view from your accounting system and CRM, calculates margin and profitability, and raises a flag when something drifts: a business line turns negative, costs run ahead of plan, or cash covers a shorter period than usual.

The agent does not pay or sign anything. It saves time on collecting numbers and shows you where to look. For the data to flow on its own, the CRM must be linked to accounting: our Bitrix24 integration with Zoho Books, QuickBooks or Odoo starts at AED 10,000. The management dashboards themselves live in BI analytics, with plans from AED 300 a month.

The procurement agent puts every quote in one table

In procurement the time goes on correspondence. You send the request, wait for replies, chase the suppliers who went quiet, and move prices from PDFs and emails into a spreadsheet. The procurement agent sends requests to suppliers, collects their quotes and compares them on price, lead time and reliability. Your buyer gets a ready comparison and spends the time negotiating instead of copying.

The costliest comparison mistakes hide in the details: different currencies, VAT included or on top, different delivery terms. The HR agent runs the first interview

A single vacancy in Dubai can attract more applications than a recruiter has time to read. The first call with each candidate takes time, and half of them drop out on basics: experience, languages, availability for the schedule, salary expectations. The HR agent takes applications from job boards and your website, runs the first interview on WhatsApp or Telegram, scores the answers and moves each candidate through the pipeline up to their first day.

Your recruiter sees a shortlist with a score for every candidate. The final interview, the decision and the offer stay with people. Candidates who are not a fit right now go into a talent pool instead of getting lost in chat threads.

Marketing and client relationships can run on agents too

Two agents sit next to sales but do not work on a specific deal. The marketing agent drafts and publishes articles and tracks how visible the company is in search and in AI assistants. The relationship agent keeps the full picture of your contacts: who is waiting for a reply, who you have not spoken to in a while, who needs a nudge. Both make sense once the core processes are already automated.

Start with the department where work repeats daily

Pick the department not by what sounds exciting but by three signs: the task repeats every day, mistakes cost money, and the result can be measured with a number. In procurement that is the time from request to supplier choice, in hiring the time to first interview, in finance how quickly the director gets the cash picture.

Before launch, make sure the agent will have something to work with:

  • finance needs access to the accounting system and CRM, plus the list of business lines to report profit on
  • procurement needs a supplier list, a request template and comparison rules: currency, VAT, lead times
  • hiring needs a job description, first-round questions and the criteria for moving a candidate forward

If agents need to work together, say procurement hands an upcoming payment to finance, they need shared data. Questions and answers

Which non-sales tasks can an AI agent take on?

A cash and risk view by business line, supplier requests and quote comparison, first interviews with candidates, article drafts and contact follow-ups. Decisions on payments, suppliers and hires stay with people.

How much does an AI agent for finance, procurement or HR cost?

One agent under the Start package costs AED 5,000 one-off, and a team of agents for several departments costs AED 10,000. Support is 10% of the implementation fee per month, prices exclude VAT.

Can the finance agent make payments?

No. The agent builds the cash picture, calculates margin and flags risks. Payments, budgets and credit remain decisions for the director and the finance team.
