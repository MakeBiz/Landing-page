AI invoice and document recognition: what it does and where it stops

Invoices, quotes, price lists and delivery notes reach you by email and WhatsApp in every possible format, and someone still types them into the CRM and the accounts. Here is what AI can pull out of documents, where it gets things wrong and how to test it on your own files.

AI document recognition extracts the supplier, number, date, amounts, VAT and line items from invoices, quotes and price lists, checks them against the purchase order and passes only the doubtful cases to a person. It stops at poor scans, handwritten notes and payment decisions, which an employee reviews. OCR reads characters, Document AI understands the document

OCR (optical character recognition) turns an image into text: letters and digits, with no meaning attached. It does not know which number is the invoice total and which is a phone number, and it needs a template for every document layout. Document AI goes further: it finds fields by meaning, rebuilds the line item table and knows that "Total incl. VAT" on one invoice and "Grand total" on another are the same field.

Results depend on the source file

The same engine gives different results on different files. This is what usually lands in a company's inbox:

  • a PDF exported from the supplier's accounting system: the text is already inside the file, which makes it the most reliable case
  • a scan: much depends on resolution and on stamps or signatures over the figures
  • a phone photo: skew, shadows, cropped edges
  • a table in an email body or a WhatsApp message: the text is there, but the structure is loose
  • an Excel price list: exact figures, but every supplier uses different columns

That is why an accuracy figure "in general" tells you very little. Until extraction has been tested on your own document flow, any number is just a number on a slide.

Invoice headers are easier than line items

Header fields appear on almost every invoice: supplier and tax registration number (TRN), document number and date, currency, total and VAT amount. Document AI handles these with confidence. Line items are harder: a table running over several pages, a description wrapped onto two lines, the same product in different pack sizes, a discount on its own line at the bottom.

Simple arithmetic helps here, and the system runs it on its own:

  • quantity times unit price equals the line amount
  • the sum of the lines equals the total before tax
  • the VAT amount matches the base at the rate stated on the document (the standard UAE rate is 5%)

If any check fails, the document is flagged and goes to a person. This is not a tax review, it is a check that the numbers were read correctly.

The line sits where a mistake costs money

High accuracy on a single field and reliability of the whole process are two different things, as we explain in our news piece on why invoices still contain errors at 98% accuracy. The practical takeaway is simple: the system has to know when it is unsure and stop, rather than write a guess into your books.

Rules we set up in almost every project:

  • PO matching: items and prices on the invoice are checked against the order, and any mismatch goes to a person
  • duplicates: same supplier, number and amount get flagged before payment
  • a new supplier or changed bank details: always reviewed by hand
  • amounts above a threshold you set: confirmed by an employee
  • payments: the AI never sends them

The result is that people work on exceptions, not on the whole document flow.

Questions and answers

How is OCR different from AI document recognition?

OCR turns an image into text but does not know where the total or the invoice number is. Document AI finds fields by meaning, rebuilds line items and checks the figures against each other.

Can AI read scans and photos?

Yes, but quality depends on the source: low resolution, stamps over figures, skew and shadows all reduce reliability. Those documents are more likely to be flagged for manual review.

Does recognition work with Arabic?

We test it on your documents before launch. Arabic text, bilingual invoices and handwritten notes behave differently depending on the file, so we do not quote accuracy percentages in advance.

Can we take people out of invoice entry completely?

You should not. The system takes the routine off the main flow, and an employee reviews PO mismatches, duplicates, new suppliers and large amounts.
