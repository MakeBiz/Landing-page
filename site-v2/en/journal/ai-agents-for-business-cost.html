AI agents for business: what is included and what it costs

The price of an AI agent is more than one number: there is the setup, the monthly support and the systems you already pay for. Here is how MakeBiz packages break down, line by line, so you know the budget before the first call.

At MakeBiz Technologies one AI agent costs AED 5,000 one-off (Start package), a team of agents across several departments costs AED 10,000 (Business package), and groups of companies get a custom quote. Support is 10% of the implementation fee per month, so AED 500 or AED 1,000. A demo on your own process is free and takes 30 to 40 minutes.

MakeBiz Technologies · 11 November 2026 · 7 min read

An agent budget has three lines

Companies usually expect one figure, but the budget has three lines: a one-off implementation fee, monthly support, and the systems that stay on your side. MakeBiz sets the first two. The third depends on the CRM, messaging channels and other tools you already run.

Implementation is paid once. It covers mapping the process, setting up the agent's role, access rights and the connection to your CRM and messaging. Support is calculated from the implementation fee, so the monthly amount is known from day one. CRM licences and your WhatsApp provider's subscription are paid directly to those vendors and are not part of our price list.

Check the CRM itself too. With no proper pipeline or randomly filled fields, the agent has nothing reliable to work with. Our standard Bitrix24 CRM setup starts at AED 10,000 with go-live from 14 days, and a WhatsApp connection is AED 500. Both are quoted separately, only if the agent needs them.

Start covers one clear job

The Start package at AED 5,000 is built around one agent on one key process. It is a low-risk entry point: you see results in one area, then decide whether to go further. The package includes a baseline, so we record how the process performs before launch and track the same numbers after.

Companies most often pick one of these agents for Start:

  • AI sales manager: replies to a client when the sales rep goes quiet and keeps deals from stalling in the pipeline
  • call agent: scores every conversation, not just the handful a manager had time to listen to
  • HR agent: runs the first interview and passes only shortlisted candidates to your recruiter

If you are choosing an agent for a sales team, start with our news piece on which agent to begin with in sales. Call scoring also needs a call speech analytics plan billed by minutes, starting at AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes.

Business is for agents that hand work to each other

The Business package at AED 10,000 is a team of agents serving several departments. The difference from Start is not just headcount. The agents share data and a dashboard, and they pass tasks along: the procurement agent collects and compares supplier quotes, and the finance agent sees the upcoming payment in the company's overall cash picture.

We roll agents out one at a time, so your team adapts and we tune each one before the next goes live. Why a single agent often hits a ceiling is covered in our news piece on teams of agents.

Holding is quoted per project because of separate environments

There is no fixed price for a group of companies, and that is not a sales trick. When there are several legal entities, each has its own data, access rights and sometimes its own CRM. A common UAE setup is a free zone company and a mainland company that must be run separately. We explained how to handle that in one portal in our news on free zone and mainland in one Bitrix24.

A Holding quote covers several environments, separated data and, if needed, your own server in the UAE. First a short assessment, then a fixed estimate, then the work.

The 10% support fee pays for ongoing tuning

An agent is never configured once and left alone. Price lists, scripts and delivery terms change, and an agent that does not know answers clients with outdated information. Support at 10% of the implementation fee per month covers three things:

  • adjusting the agent to changes in your process and data
  • investigating failures: why the agent gave the wrong answer or did not answer at all
  • improvements: new scenarios that come up after the first weeks in production

For Start that is AED 500 a month, for Business AED 1,000. It does not depend on the number of chats or users, which makes it easy to plan into an annual budget.

Your contract is with MakeBiz Technologies FZE LLC and invoices are issued in AED. The estimate is fixed before work begins, so the amount does not drift during the project. Prices exclude VAT and licences. Access and data stay with you: the portal, the server and the accounts belong to your company.

Questions and answers

How much does one AI agent cost?

Implementing one agent under the Start package costs AED 5,000 one-off, with support at AED 500 a month. Prices exclude VAT and CRM licences.

What does AI agent support include?

Adjusting the agent to changes in your process, investigating failures and improving scenarios. It costs 10% of the implementation fee per month and does not depend on chat volume.

Can I start with one agent and add more later?

Yes, that is exactly what Start is for. Once the first agent is live and the numbers show a result, we add the next ones, with scope and price fixed in an estimate in advance.

Are there costs on top of the package?

CRM licences and the WhatsApp provider subscription are paid to those vendors directly. If the agent needs a CRM setup or an integration first, we list that work in the estimate before we start.

Where is the data the agent works with stored?

Access and data stay with you: the portal, the server and the accounts belong to your company. For a group of companies the agents can run on your own server in the UAE.

How do I know if an agent fits my business?

Book a demo and in 30 to 40 minutes we will show an agent working on your own process. It is free and there is no commitment.
