Questions and answers

How much does Bitrix24 implementation cost in the UAE?

Standard Bitrix24 CRM setup from AED 10,000, basic launch from 14 days. Work beyond the standard is AED 200 per hour. The Bitrix24 license is paid separately: cloud from AED 180 a month billed annually, on-premise from AED 13,184 a year.

How much does Bitrix24 support cost?

Monthly hour packages: 5 hours for AED 1,250, 10 hours for AED 2,300, 20 hours for AED 4,200. An hour beyond the package is AED 250.

How much do AI agents cost?

One agent from AED 5,000. Packages: Start AED 5,000, Business AED 10,000, Holding priced individually. Maintenance is 10% of the implementation cost per month, the demo is free.

How much do integrations, WhatsApp and BI cost?

Accounting integration (Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Odoo, 1C) from AED 10,000, WhatsApp or another messenger connection AED 500, BI analytics from AED 1,000. The estimate is fixed before start.

How much does call speech analytics cost?

AED 1,000 a month for 5,000 minutes, AED 1,700 for 10,000 minutes, from AED 4,000 for 30,000 minutes and more.
