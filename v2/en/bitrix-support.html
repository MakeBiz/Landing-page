Questions and answers

How much does Bitrix24 support cost?

Monthly hour packages: 5 hours for AED 1,250, 10 hours for AED 2,300, 20 hours for AED 4,200. An hour beyond the package is AED 250.

Will you take a portal set up by others?

Yes. We start with an audit: how processes, permissions and integrations are set up, and what to fix first.

What if the hours run out early?

You buy extra hours at AED 250 or move to a bigger package. Before a large task we give an estimate in hours.

How fast do you respond?

We take requests in a work chat on WhatsApp or Telegram, reply within a business day, urgent failures go first.

Can we see where the hours went?

Yes, every task and the time spent is logged openly, at the end of the month we send a report.
