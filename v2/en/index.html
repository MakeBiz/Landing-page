Questions and answers

What does MakeBiz do in the UAE?

Two directions. First: Bitrix24 implementation and support for companies in Dubai and across the Emirates, including WhatsApp, accounting integrations, licenses and BI analytics. Second: AI agents that take over routine in sales, finance, procurement and hiring.

Are you an official Bitrix24 partner?

Yes, MakeBiz Technologies is an official Bitrix24 partner in the UAE: we implement and support portals and help with cloud and on-premise Bitrix24 licenses.

How much does Bitrix24 implementation cost?

Standard Bitrix24 CRM setup starts from AED 10,000. Work beyond the standard is charged at AED 200 per hour, with a fixed estimate after a short survey. The Bitrix24 license is paid separately.

How much do AI agents cost?

One agent on your process starts from AED 5,000, maintenance is 10% of the implementation cost per month. A demo on your process is free.

Can you connect WhatsApp to Bitrix24?

Yes. Connecting WhatsApp or another messenger costs AED 500, the provider subscription is paid separately. Every message creates a deal and lands in the client card.

Do you work only in Dubai?

We work with companies across all Emirates, meetings in Dubai or by video call. We speak English and Russian, documents and invoices in dirhams.
