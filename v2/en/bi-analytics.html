Questions and answers

What is BI analytics in Bitrix24?

Management dashboards on data from the CRM and other systems: sales, pipeline, manager performance, money in dirhams. We build them in the Bitrix24 BI builder or an external BI system.

Which sources can be connected?

Bitrix24, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Odoo, 1C, telephony, Google Ads, Meta, Google Analytics, spreadsheets and bank statements. The set depends on your questions.

How much does BI analytics cost?

BI analytics starts from AED 1,000. The final price depends on the number of sources and metrics, the estimate is fixed after we agree on the metric list.

Will the numbers be right?

We start by checking CRM data: a dashboard tells the truth only if the system has no garbage. If needed, we first clean up cards and stages.
