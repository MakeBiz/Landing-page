An agent, not a chatbot

Chatbot

  • Answers by script and forgets the conversation
  • Lives on the website, does not see the CRM
  • Does not finish the task
  • Waits until someone comes

AI agent

  • Remembers the client and the history
  • Works in the CRM, WhatsApp, accounting and the bank
  • Runs the process to the result
  • Notices a problem and brings in a person

Questions and answers

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers a question and forgets it. An agent has a role, memory and access to your systems: it runs a process to the result, for example takes over a chat if a manager has not replied.

How much does AI agent implementation cost?

One agent from AED 5,000. Packages: Start AED 5,000, Business AED 10,000, Holding priced individually. Maintenance is 10% of the implementation cost per month. A demo on your process is free.

Where do the agents work?

In WhatsApp, Telegram and your CRM. The system is deployed on your server, including in the UAE, access stays with you.

Which systems do agents work with?

Bitrix24, WhatsApp, Telegram, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Odoo, 1C, bank statements, telephony, Google Ads, Google Analytics, Search Console. Others on request.

Do the agents work in English?

Yes. Agents talk to clients in their language (English, Russian, Arabic where needed) and count in dirhams.

Where do we start?

With one agent where there is the most routine or loss: chats, calls, hiring or procurement. We launch, measure and add the next ones.
