Questions and answers

How is the liaison agent different from a CRM?

A CRM stores deals. The liaison agent watches people: who is waiting, who you have not talked to for a while, what is agreed for today, and passes to the CRM what becomes a deal.

Where does the agent get contacts?

From WhatsApp, Telegram, Bitrix24 and email. The source set is defined at the start.

Who is it for?

Owners, managers and salespeople with hundreds of contacts and agreements that must not be lost.
