Channel report

WhatsApp rules to know in advance

The 24-hour window

You can reply in free text within 24 hours after the client's last message. When the window closes, you can write first only with a template approved by Meta: a reminder, order status, an invoice

Templates and broadcasts

Template messages are paid, the provider sets the price. Broadcasts only to people who opted in, otherwise complaints grow and the number gets limited

Questions and answers

Can WhatsApp be connected to Bitrix24?

Yes. We connect the company number through the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta and a connector in the Bitrix24 Contact Center. Conversations land in the deal card, managers reply right from the CRM.

How much does it cost?

Connecting WhatsApp or another messenger costs AED 500. The provider subscription and paid template messages are separate, we calculate the costs before connecting.

Which channels do you connect?

WhatsApp, Telegram, social media messages, website live chat and email. All conversations in one Bitrix24 window.

Why not via the web version QR code?

That connection is unofficial, with broadcasts and complaints the sales number can be blocked. For the main number we use only the official API.

Will the history stay if a manager leaves?

Yes, the whole history stays in the client card in Bitrix24, not on a personal phone.
